Choose the radar(s) fitted to your sensor (radar 2 is the second LD2450 socket on dual-radar boards), connect it with a USB data cable, click Install firmware and choose the serial port.

Getting started

  1. InstallFlash the firmware above, or skip this step if your sensor came pre-installed.
  2. Connect to Wi-FiOn your phone, join the pro-sensor-xxxxxx Wi-Fi network. The setup page opens automatically (or go to 192.168.4.1); pick your network and enter the password.
  3. Add to Home AssistantIt's discovered automatically under Settings → Devices & services. Click Add.
  4. Choose your remotesUnder the device's Configuration, set TV remote, AC remote and Fan remote to your brands.

Good to know

  • Updates: sensors in Home Assistant update themselves. Look for the Firmware update entity.
  • Factory reset: unplug and replug the power 5 times, each replug within 10 seconds.
  • Alarms: follows Alarmo automatically, and the External alarm switch works with any automation.
  • Safety: the high-temperature alert is not a certified smoke or fire alarm.
